FEATURES

Built to stay chill

SAF is a free economy SMP — claim land, run a shop, team up with people you trust, and only fight when both sides say yes.

Land Claims & Anti-Grief

Claim and protect your base with in-game land claims. Go build somewhere without worrying about coming back to a hole in the ground.

Java & Bedrock, Same World

Cross-play means PC, console, and mobile all drop into the same economy. One IP, one port, no launcher wrangling.

Player-Driven Economy

Set up shop, trade with other players, and build up your own corner of the market. Currency and prices are shaped by the crew, not a plugin default.

Teams, Not Warfare

Group up with people you trust. It's peaceful by default — PvP only happens when both sides agree to it.

Backed Up, Not Fragile

The world gets backed up regularly, so a bad crash doesn't mean losing what you've built.

Chill, Low-Drama Community

No forced PvP, no surprise raids. Show up decent, build what you want, and trade at your own pace.

GROUND RULES

Keep it simple

Nothing complicated — just enough so the economy stays fair and the world stays chill.

  • 01

    Claim your base. Anything outside a claim is fair game, so don't leave something valuable unclaimed.

  • 02

    Peaceful by default. No unauthorized killing. Fighting, raiding, and PvP only happen when everyone involved has agreed to it.

  • 03

    No griefing and avoid toxicity. Be kind, show some respect.

  • 04

    No cheats or hacked clients. Play it straight — exploits and glitch abuse ruin it for everyone else, including the economy.

  • 05

    Report bugs and dupes. instead of using them. We'd rather fix it than ban someone over it.

  • 06

    Be a decent hang. Trade fair, treat teammates well, and keep it chill for everyone else grinding alongside you.
