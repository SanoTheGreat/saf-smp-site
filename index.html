Features

Built to stay chill

Claim land, run a shop, team up with people you trust, and only fight when both sides say yes.

Land Claims & Anti-Grief

Claim and protect your base with in-game land claims. Build without worrying about coming back to a hole in the ground.

Java & Bedrock, Same World

Cross-play means PC, console and mobile all drop into the same economy. No launcher wrangling.

Player-Driven Economy

Set up shop, trade with other players and build your own corner of the market. Prices are shaped by the crew, not a plugin default.

Teams, Not Warfare

Group up with people you trust. It's peaceful by default: PvP only happens when both sides agree to it.

Backed Up, Not Fragile

The world gets backed up regularly, so a bad crash doesn't mean losing what you've built.

Chill, Low-Drama Community

No forced PvP, no surprise raids. Show up decent, build what you want and trade at your own pace.

Ground Rules

Keep it simple

Just enough so the economy stays fair and the world stays chill.

  • 01

    Claim your base. Anything outside a claim is fair game, so don't leave something valuable unclaimed.

  • 02

    Peaceful by default. No unauthorized killing. Fighting, raiding and PvP only happen when everyone involved has agreed to it.

  • 03

    No griefing, no toxicity. Be kind and show some respect.

  • 04

    No cheats or hacked clients. Exploits and glitch abuse ruin it for everyone else, including the economy.

  • 05

    Report bugs and dupes. Don't use them. We'd rather fix it than ban someone over it.

  • 06

    Be a decent hang. Trade fair, treat teammates well and keep it chill for everyone grinding alongside you.

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is SAF free to play?

Yes. The server is free and open to everyone. Ranks in the store are optional perks that help keep the server running.

Do I need to apply or get whitelisted?

No. There's no whitelist and no application. Add the server and join.

Can Bedrock players join? (phone, console, Windows)

Yes. Java and Bedrock players share the same world. On Bedrock, use the address play.safsmp.online with port 26117.

Can other players attack me or raid my base?

PvP only happens when both sides agree to it. Claimed land is protected, but anything outside a claim is fair game, so claim your base early.

How do I buy a rank or Ecoins?

Join the Discord and open a support ticket. Staff will send payment details and give you your purchase in-game. See the store for the full steps.

I found a bug or a dupe. What should I do?

Report it in the Discord instead of using it. We'd rather fix it than ban someone over it.
