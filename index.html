FEATURES

Built for a small crew, not a crowd

SAF isn't trying to be a network. It's one curated Bedrock world, set up so it's actually still standing next time you log in.

Land Claims & Anti-Grief

Claim and protect your base with in-game land claims. Go build somewhere without worrying about coming back to a hole in the ground.

Play On Any Platform

Bedrock Edition means Windows, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, and mobile all drop into the same world. No launcher wrangling, no downloads.

Biomes to Explore

From sunbaked deserts to the Nether's fire, the full spread of vanilla biomes is out there waiting — enough to make exploring worth it again.

Friendly by Default

This is a friends-and-friends-of-friends server, not a public free-for-all. Show up decent and you'll fit right in.

Backed Up, Not Fragile

The world gets backed up regularly, so a bad crash doesn't mean losing what you've built.

Events, When We Feel Like It

Seasonal builds, group projects, and the occasional boss fight whenever enough of the crew is online for it.

THE MAP

A whole frontier to spread out into

Plains for your first base, mountains for a stronghold, the Nether and the End for the ones who want more. Pick a biome and start claiming.

Plains

Wide open grass, easy building, the classic first-day spawn.

Forest

Dense canopy, plenty of wood, and somewhere to lose the crowd.

Desert

Sand for miles, hidden temples, and cacti that will absolutely hurt you.

Ocean

Coral reefs, shipwrecks, and whatever's waiting in the deep dark water.

Mountains

Snow caps and stone cliffs — a natural, half-built stronghold.

The Nether

Fire, fortresses, and the fastest way to travel long distances — if you survive.

The End

Void, islands, and a dragon that's still undefeated. For now.

Swamp

Murky water, witch huts, and mangroves worth the mud on your boots.

GROUND RULES

Keep it simple

Nothing complicated — just enough so the world stays good for everyone.

  • 01

    Claim your base. Anything outside a claim is fair game, so don't leave something valuable unclaimed.

  • 02

    No Griefing and Avoid Toxicity. Be kind show some respect

  • 03

    No cheats or hacked clients. Play it straight — exploits and glitch abuse ruin it for everyone else.

  • 04

    Report bugs and dupes. instead of using them. We'd rather fix it than ban someone over it.

  • 05

    Be a decent hang. It's a small server — treat it like you're a guest in someone's house, because you are.
